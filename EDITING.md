# Editing the lab website

Everything is done in a web browser. You can't break the design by editing content.

## Getting in

1. Go to **https://app.pagescms.org** and click **Sign in with GitHub**.
2. Open the lab website repository.
3. Use the menu on the left: **News, People, Research areas, Publications, Join page, Homepage & site settings.**

After you click **Save**, the live site updates on its own within about 15–20 minutes.

## Common tasks

**Post news**
News → add a new entry → fill in the headline, date, and a one- or two-sentence summary. An image and longer story are optional. Save. The three most recent items appear on the homepage automatically.

**Add a new lab member**
People → add a new entry → name, role, department/program, and a photo (square photos look best). Save.
*Sort order* controls who appears first (lower numbers first).

**When someone graduates or leaves**
Open their entry → turn on **Alumni** → add their years in the lab and current position. Save. They move from the team grid to the alumni list.

**Update publications**
1. Export your papers as BibTeX: in Google Scholar, go to your profile, tick the select-all box, then **Export → BibTeX**; or in Zotero, select the papers and choose **Export → BibTeX**.
2. Publications → select all the text, paste the new export over it, Save.
To pin a paper to the homepage, add a line `featured = {true},` inside that paper's entry. Otherwise the homepage shows the most recent papers.

**Change homepage text**
Homepage & site settings → edit the headline, intro, "How we measure" list, and contact details.

**Edit a research area**
Research areas → pick one → edit the summary (shown on the card) and the full description (shown on its own page). You can add an image and caption. One area can be marked **Flagship** to get the large blue card.

## Removing things

To remove a person or news item, open it and delete it. For people who have left, marking them as alumni is usually better than deleting.
