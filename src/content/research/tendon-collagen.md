Collagen is the load-bearing backbone of tendon, cervix, uterus, and many other tissues. We relate measured fiber architecture to mechanical behavior using fiber-reinforced constitutive models and image-informed inverse finite element analysis, with the goal of models that carry over from one collagen-rich tissue to another.
