Large bone defects do not heal on their own. We design calcium phosphate scaffolds with controlled architecture, from macropore geometry down to microporosity, and study how that architecture and the addition of biologics guide new bone into the scaffold.

To quantify regeneration, we use micro-CT with deep-learning segmentation to separate scaffold, new bone, and soft tissue in three dimensions.
