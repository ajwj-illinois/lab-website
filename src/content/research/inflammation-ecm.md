Inflammation and matrix remodeling are tightly coupled in reproductive tissues, but they are usually studied separately. As investigators in Biohub Chicago, we are developing ways to image inflammation, estrogen signaling, and the fibrous extracellular matrix together, in space and over time.

We pair SHG imaging and mechanical testing with spatial transcriptomics, so that gene expression can be read directly against tissue structure and mechanics.
