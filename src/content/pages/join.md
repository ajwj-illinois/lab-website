## Graduate students

Prospective PhD students should apply through the [Department of Mechanical Science and Engineering](https://mechse.illinois.edu) or a related graduate program, and mention the lab in their application. Feel free to email a CV and a short note about your interests.

## Postdoctoral researchers

Please email a CV, a brief statement of research interests, and the names of two or three references.

## Undergraduates

Undergraduates interested in research should email a short note describing their interests, relevant coursework, and availability.
