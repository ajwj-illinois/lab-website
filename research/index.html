Research

One lens, several systems

Collagen-rich tissues and engineered materials share a common language: structure, mechanics, and remodeling. We study that language across scales.

Flagship area

Reproductive tissue mechanics & preterm birth

The cervix and uterus remodel dramatically during pregnancy. We measure how their collagen architecture and mechanical properties change, and what goes wrong in spontaneous preterm birth, linking tissue microstructure to clinical outcomes.

CervixUterusECM remodelingCERES

Inflammation & the extracellular matrix

As part of Biohub Chicago, we image inflammation, hormonal signaling, and fibrous ECM together in space and time to understand how tissues sense and respond to injury.

Biohub ChicagoSpatial transcriptomics

Scaffolds for bone regeneration

We design and 3D-print calcium phosphate scaffolds and study how geometry, porosity, and biologics drive new bone formation in large-animal models.

Calcium phosphateMicro-CTDeep learning

Engineered substrates for coral restoration

Borrowing from biomaterials, we engineer surfaces that encourage coral larvae to settle and survive, a key bottleneck in reef restoration.

Larval settlementSurface design

Tendon & collagen mechanics

We connect collagen fiber organization to tissue-level behavior, building constitutive models that generalize across collagen-rich tissues.
